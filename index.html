02 · Projects

ParkLog

Android + iOS · React Native

A Pokédex for cars. Take a photo of a car on the street and the app suggests the brand, model and year with AI, scores its rarity, pins where you saw it and unlocks achievements. Built in about a week; works offline, no account needed, and your data stays on your phone.

  • AI without exposing the key: the model is called through a Cloudflare Worker and the app validates the response again
  • AI suggests, it doesn't decide: recognition is opt-in, has confidence levels and can be undone
  • 173 unit tests on the logic (rarity, validation, backups, achievements), run on GitHub Actions
  • Safe data: .zip backups with photos, treated as untrusted input on import
  • 116 brands, rarity engine, map, 50 achievements and a PT/EN interface

Smart Growing Monitor

Internship · Flutter

A mobile app for monitoring smart growing systems, built during my internship at an agricultural automation company. I took over an unstable Flutter prototype, fixed it, added new features and got it through Google Play review.

  • Talks to real hardware over TCP and UDP on the local network to show live sensor data
  • Sensor values now instant, down from 5–10 seconds, with fixed charts and clear error handling
  • Google sign-in, device unlock, light/dark themes and PT/EN/ES translations
  • Beta tested and approved in Google Play's review
